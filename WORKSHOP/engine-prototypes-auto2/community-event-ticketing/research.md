# Research: community-event-ticketing

## Search 1: Humanitix — Australian social enterprise ticketing

**Humanitix** (humanitix.com) is an Australian-founded event ticketing platform structured as a social enterprise. 100% of profits are donated to charity (education, healthcare, basic necessities). Founded in Sydney. Has donated $16.5M+ to charity to date.

**Features that directly match this outcome:**
- Create events with details, dates, capacity ✅
- Attendee registration/RSVP for free events ✅ (free events = $0 fees)
- Ticket purchasing for paid events ✅
- Attendee management: check-in (scanning app), waitlists ✅
- Email campaigns/reminders ✅
- No data mining — social enterprise model, not ad-funded ✅
- Beautiful design customisation ✅
- Discount codes, bulk upload, rich reporting ✅

**Pricing:**
- Free events: $0 (completely free)
- Standard paid tickets: 4% + $0.99 per ticket (excl. GST)
- Charities & Schools: 2.5% + $0.50 per ticket
- No sign-up fees, no contracts, no subscription
- ALL plans get ALL features

**Values alignment:** Social enterprise. Australian. Profits go to charity, not shareholders. Not VC-extractive. Explicitly serves charities, schools, community groups.

## Search 2: TryBooking — Australian event ticketing

**TryBooking** (trybooking.com) is an Australian event ticketing platform specifically popular with community groups, schools, and associations.

**Features:**
- Event creation with full details ✅
- Registration events (no ticket required) ✅
- Paid ticketing ✅
- Scanning app for check-in ✅
- Custom forms for data collection ✅
- Free events are completely free ✅

**Pricing:**
- Free events: $0
- Paid events: 50c per ticket (or 15c if ticket ≤ $5) + 2.5% processing fee
- No subscriptions, no tiered features

**Values alignment:** Australian-owned. Simple, low-cost. Popular with community organisations. No data mining business model.

## Search 3: Pretix — open source self-hosted ticketing

**Pretix** (pretix.eu) is an open-source (Apache 2.0) event ticketing system. German-based. Self-hostable.

**Features:**
- Full ticketing: online shop, box office, ticket outlets ✅
- Privacy-focused, ISO 27001 certified ✅
- Used for conferences, festivals, workshops ✅
- Self-hosted community edition is free

**Pricing:**
- Self-hosted Community: Free
- Hosted: From €149/month
- Enterprise: Custom

**Trade-offs:** More complex to set up. European-focused. Less suited to small community events than Humanitix/TryBooking. Better for conferences and large events.

## Key Finding

Multiple excellent tools exist for community event ticketing in Australia:

1. **Humanitix** — Best values-aligned option. Australian social enterprise. Free for free events, low fees for paid. Profits go to charity. Purpose-built for this.
2. **TryBooking** — Simplest and cheapest. Australian. Very popular with community groups already.
3. **Pretix** — Best for data sovereignty (self-hosted, open source). More setup effort.

Building a new ticketing system would be redundant. Humanitix in particular is almost uniquely well-suited: it's Australian, social enterprise, donates to charity, free for free events, low fees, and explicitly serves community organizations. Building a competitor to this makes no sense — you'd be competing with a charity.
