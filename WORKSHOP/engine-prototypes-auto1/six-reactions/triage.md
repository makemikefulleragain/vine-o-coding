# Triage: six-reactions

## Find → Connect → Extend → Integrate → Build

### Find
No platform implements value-type reactions (Fact/Fun/Spicy/Nice/Curious/Surprising). Facebook, LinkedIn, and Discord have multi-reaction systems but they categorise emotions, not content value types.

### Connect
The engagement research confirms multi-reaction systems are superior to binary like/dislike. LinkedIn's "Insightful" reaction is the closest single-reaction precedent. The "room character" analytics concept (aggregating reactions to show what kind of conversation a room has) is novel.

### Extend
Nothing to extend. This is an original design from kamunity.ai's principles.

### Integrate
N/A.

### Build
**Decision: BUILD.** This is kamunity.ai's own concept with no existing implementation to adopt. The research validates the broader approach (multi-reaction > binary), and the specific taxonomy is original. Building a React component with animations and room-level analytics is warranted.

## Triage Result: BUILD
Justification: Genuinely novel concept. Research supports multi-reaction systems broadly. No existing tool to recommend.
