💡 Zone View: Same filtered entities grouped by zone. Each zone shows entity count + average impact score. Good for weekly check-in — scan each zone column and spot gaps or overloads.
💡 Tasks: Your full task list grouped by priority. Checkboxes save state to localStorage. Critical tasks always surface first. "Draft Email" on outreach tasks opens a Waymaker comms draft.
💡 Safety: Your threat model, tracked here. Click a status badge to cycle it: open → in-progress → done. Critical items surface first. Nothing gets forgotten, everything is honest.
💡 Gaps: Known unknowns and open questions. Honest self-assessment. Toggle “Mark resolved” when you’ve answered a question or closed a gap. Gaps = systemic issues. Questions = things you haven’t figured out yet.

💡 Roadmap: Live view of your PHASE_QUEUE.md — every phase with progress bar, open items, and pre-chewed Waymaker/Opus prompts. Filter by Active, Complete, or Next. Data lives in control.js PHASES array — update it to keep the queue honest.
💡 Detail panel: Full entity profile — description, tags, roadmap note, URL, effort/impact scores. In the full build this will be editable, saving back to data.js or a sync file that Waymaker and Claude Desktop can also read.
