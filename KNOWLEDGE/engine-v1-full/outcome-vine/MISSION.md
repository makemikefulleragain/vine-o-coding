# MISSION.md — Outcome Vine Coding

## Strategic Outcome

**Enable anyone with a community need to build their own digital tools using AI coding assistants — through a transparent, evidence-based methodology that grows real solutions one phase at a time.**

This is local liberation: communities shouldn't need to wait for a tech company, hire a developer, or learn to code from scratch. They can describe what they need, follow a structured process, and build it themselves with AI as their pair programmer.

## Why This Matters

AI coding assistants (Windsurf, Cursor, GitHub Copilot) have crossed a threshold: a person with no programming experience can now build and deploy real software. But most people who try end up with:

- A demo that falls apart when they try to use it for real
- An app that works once but can't be extended or maintained
- A pile of code they don't understand and can't fix when it breaks
- Or they never start, because "build me an app" is too vague to produce anything useful

The gap isn't "can AI write code?" — it's **"how do I tell the AI what to build, and how do I keep the project on track as it grows?"**

Outcome Vine Coding fills that gap. It provides:
1. A set of **foundation documents** that define the project before any code is written
2. A **phase loop** that grows the solution through research-informed, evidence-tested iterations
3. **Safety mechanisms** (Review & Reflect, confidence scoring, kill switch) that catch problems early
4. A **human-AI collaboration model** where the human owns the outcome and the AI does the building

## What Exists (Starting Point)

The methodology was developed through the Community Grants Hub project — a real tool for Australian nonprofit treasurers, built from scratch in a single session using Windsurf. The grants-hub:

- Went through 8 phases (including a full revert and rebuild after Phase 2 failed)
- Produced a deployed, working tool at https://grants-hub.netlify.app
- Generated complete documentation of every research decision, triage choice, and failure recovery
- Demonstrated that the process works — and that the safety mechanisms (R&R) catch real problems

All of this is the raw material for the site.

## What "Done" Looks Like (North Star)

A person with a community need can:
1. **Find** the site when searching for "how to build an app with AI" or similar
2. **Understand** the Outcome Vine Coding methodology in under 5 minutes
3. **Use the widget** to generate their foundation documents (CONSTITUTION, MISSION, RUNNER, PHASE_QUEUE, SETUP, STATE) from their answers to guided questions
4. **Copy the prompt**, paste it into Windsurf (or another AI coding assistant), and start building
5. **Follow the case study** (grants-hub) to see what the process looks like in practice
6. **Go deeper** (Brian's lean-in sections) if they want to understand the theory

This is a north star, not a Phase 1 target.

## Who This Is For

**Primary: Maya** — a 9th grader who has an idea for her school cooking club. She's never written code. She's heard of AI but doesn't know what a "prompt" is in a coding context. She needs the site to hold her hand through the widget and produce something she can use immediately.

**Secondary: Brian** — a 3rd year university student studying IT or design. He's built some things but wants a methodology, not just vibes. He wants to understand why phases are hypotheses, how confidence scoring prevents scope creep, what an Andon cord is and why Review & Reflect matters. He'll use the lean-in sections and the case study.

**Tertiary: Community enablers** — teachers, nonprofit workers, community centre staff, local council digital officers who want to help others in their community build tools. They might run a workshop using this site.

**Not for:** Professional developers (they have their own workflows). Enterprise teams (different scale, different needs). People looking for a no-code platform (this IS coding, with AI assistance).

## The Broader Vision

Outcome Vine Coding is one tool from Kamunity (kamunity.ai) — a room-based community platform where communities come together. The grants-hub is one of Kamunity's tools. This methodology site is another. They share a belief: **communities are stronger when they can build their own solutions.**

## What This Is NOT

- Not a coding tutorial (there are plenty of those)
- Not a prompt engineering guide (it's about project structure, not prompt tricks)
- Not a no-code platform (the output is real code, built by AI, owned by the user)
- Not tied to Windsurf specifically (the methodology works with any AI coding assistant, though examples use Windsurf)
- It IS the bridge between "I have a need" and "I have a working, deployed tool"
