# Iteration 02 — Directional Micro-Spec

**Date:** 2025-01-27
**Theme:** Coordination Support Gap — Capacity First, Then Tools
**Reframe origin:** Human decision after Iteration 01 escalation
**Cross-cutting Constraint:** Trust
**Status:** Directional only — NOT a build spec

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Community groups and small-to-medium NFPs in Australia face a **Coordination Support Gap**: they struggle to coordinate effectively not because good digital tools don't exist, but because they lack the **strategic capacity, digital literacy, facilitation skills, and governance frameworks** to use any tools well.

This gap manifests as:
- Only 20% of small-medium NFPs have an IT plan/budget
- Only 1/3 report their IT systems work well
- 76% adopt AI tools while foundational infrastructure is missing
- Fragmented systems, no digital roadmap, weak cybersecurity
- No shared frameworks for choosing, configuring, or governing coordination tools

The gap is **not primarily technological.** Building or recommending a new platform without addressing the underlying capacity deficit would repeat a well-documented pattern of tool-first thinking that this research warns against.

---

## 2. What "Done" Looks Like (Directionally)

If KomUnity addressed the Coordination Support Gap, a community group would be able to:

1. **Assess their coordination needs** using a simple, non-technical framework — understanding what they're trying to coordinate before choosing tools
2. **Evaluate existing tools** against their specific needs, governance values, and trust requirements — rather than adopting whatever is popular or free
3. **Access peer support** from other community groups who have navigated similar challenges — a community of practice, not a product
4. **Implement basic governance** for their digital tools — who controls the data, how decisions are made, what happens when things go wrong
5. **Build digital literacy progressively** — starting from where they are (even if that's "just email"), not where a platform vendor wants them to be

This is explicitly **NOT** a software product spec. It is a capacity-building program spec with potential digital components.

---

## 3. Triage Check: What Already Exists?

### 3a. Existing Solutions That Partially Address This

| Solution | What It Does | What's Missing |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Infoxchange Digital Transformation Hub** | Practical guides, NFP tech discounts, expert advice for AU sector | Focuses on organisational transformation, not community coordination specifically; top-down, not peer-led |
| **Hylo + facilitation model** | Bundles prosocial facilitation with platform; teaches community stewardship | Bay Area only; no AU presence; requires adoption of Hylo platform |
| **NTEN** | Nonprofit technology capacity building, community of practice | North America focused; not community-group-level |
| **Loomio** | Governance and decision-making tool (NZ-based) | Tool-specific, not a broader coordination support framework |
| **Community Houses / Neighbourhood Centres** | Physical spaces for community coordination, peer support | Exist but digital capacity varies enormously; no shared digital framework |

### 3b. Triage Verdict

**Partial coverage exists.** Infoxchange covers organisational digital transformation. Hylo bundles facilitation with a platform. NTEN provides peer learning. Community Houses provide physical infrastructure.

**What's missing:** A **community-of-practice model** specifically for Australian grassroots groups that:
- Starts with coordination needs assessment (not tool selection)
- Centres trust and governance as prerequisites
- Is peer-led (not vendor-led or government-led)
- Bridges the gap between physical community spaces and digital coordination
- Respects Indigenous data sovereignty and diverse digital literacy levels

### 3c. Build/Adopt/Contribute Decision

**Recommendation: NEITHER BUILD NOR ADOPT YET.**

Before any build/adopt decision:
1. **Listen first:** Conduct structured conversations with Australian community practitioners (Community Houses Victoria, WACOSS members, Volunteering WA, Aboriginal community-controlled organisations)
2. **Map the ecosystem:** Document what coordination support already exists in AU at the grassroots level
3. **Pilot the capacity-building model:** Test a lightweight coordination needs assessment framework with 3-5 willing community groups
4. **Then evaluate:** Whether existing tools (Hylo, Loomio, mainstream options) meet the needs that emerge, or whether something new is warranted

## 4. Trust Constraint Applied

Every element of this spec is tested against the Trust constraint:

| Spec Element | Trust Test | Pass? |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Needs assessment first | Respects community self-determination; doesn't impose solutions | Yes |
| Peer-led model | Trust built through relationships, not authority | Yes |
| Governance literacy | Enables communities to evaluate trust architecture of any platform | Yes |
| Listen-first approach | Prevents assumptions about what communities need | Yes |
| No build yet | Avoids creating something communities haven't asked for | Yes |
| Progressive digital literacy | Meets people where they are; doesn't create new exclusions | Yes |

---

## 5. What This Spec Does NOT Solve

- **It does not specify a platform.** That is intentional — the research says capacity before tools.
- **It does not address Indigenous data sovereignty in detail.** We lack the voices and authority to spec this. It requires direct engagement with Aboriginal community-controlled organisations.
- **It does not guarantee funding or sustainability.** A capacity-building program needs its own sustainability model.
- **It does not resolve the central tension.** It sequences it: capacity first, then tool evaluation. The tension may resolve differently for different communities.

---

## 6. Recommended Next Steps (for Iteration 03+)

1. **Community listening:** Design a structured conversation guide for Australian community practitioners. Target: 5-10 conversations.
2. **Ecosystem mapping:** Document existing coordination support in AU (beyond what web search can find).
3. **Hylo evaluation:** If community listening reveals coordination tool needs that Hylo addresses, conduct a hands-on evaluation with an Australian test group.
4. **Governance framework draft:** Create a lightweight "governance health check" that community groups can use to evaluate any platform.
5. **Capacity building pilot:** Test a peer-led coordination support model with willing community groups.
