## The Queue

### Phase 1: a simple place to post about the things they made and ask questions and search lists of local groups, on-line or in-person or sources to find patterns and people
**Goal:** a simple place to post about the things they made and ask questions and search lists of local groups, on-line or in-person or sources to find patterns and people
**Why first:** This is the most important thing to get right before anything else.

### Phase 2: Gallery, Appreciation & Mobile Polish
**Goal:** Let members showcase their creations in a gallery view, add "hearts" to appreciate posts, and fix mobile navigation with a hamburger menu.
**Why second:** Builds the social/showcase foundation needed before selling. Hearts give creators feedback and encouragement. Mobile polish makes the site usable for the majority of casual users.

### Phase 3: Selling via External Platforms
**Goal:** Add "available for sale" flags to creations with links to external payment platforms (Etsy, PayPal.me, Ko-fi). Research legal/transparency requirements. Do NOT build payment processing — connect to what exists.
**Why third:** The original Phase 2 ("rate and sell") was too broad. Splitting selling into its own phase allows proper research into legal, trust, and transparency requirements from the constitution.

### Phase 4: Other Crafts, Events & Meetup Coordination
**Goal:** Include other crafts and art (painting, etc.), add an events/meetup board for coordinating galleries, festivals, and hangouts.
**Why fourth:** Fariha also paints and dreams of the gallery circuit. This phase expands beyond knitting/crochet and adds event coordination.

### Phase 5+: Expand Based on Evidence
**Goal:** Whatever research and user feedback from earlier phases reveals as the next priority. Possible areas: user accounts, cross-device sync, community governance/decision-making, membership/cost-sharing model.
**Why last:** By this point, real users should be using the tool and providing feedback. Build what they actually need.
