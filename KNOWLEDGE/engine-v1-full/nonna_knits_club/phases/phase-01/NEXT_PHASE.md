## Recommended Phase 2 Focus

The PHASE_QUEUE.md says Phase 2 is: **"rate and sell them to people"**

### Refinement Based on Phase 1 Learning

Phase 2 as stated is too broad and involves complex territory (payments, legal). Recommend splitting:

**Phase 2a: Gallery & Showcase**
- Let members create a simple profile/gallery of their creations
- Add the ability to "heart" or appreciate posts
- This builds the foundation for selling without the complexity of payments

**Phase 2b: Selling & Marketplace Connections**
- Connect to existing platforms (Etsy, PayPal, Ko-fi) rather than building payment processing
- Add "available for sale" flag to gallery items with external purchase links
- Research the legal/transparency requirements from the constitution

### Why This Split?

1. Selling involves money → trust → legal → complexity. Rushing it risks harm.
2. A gallery/showcase is valuable on its own and tests whether members want to share more.
3. The constitution explicitly warns about financial features needing safeguards.
