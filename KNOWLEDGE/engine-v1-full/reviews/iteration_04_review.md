## The Model Amendment Requiring Review

### Previous Model (I01-03, supported by 6+ sources)
"Capacity first, then tools."

### Amended Model (I04)
"Capacity first for communities learning to coordinate digitally. Build when existing platforms are extractive by design. In both cases, trust architecture is the foundation."

### Evidence for Amendment
**Single primary source:** The Pack Music Co-operative is correctly building a new streaming platform because Spotify's prorated payment model is structurally extractive for independent local artists. No capacity building on Spotify addresses this structural problem. Their Triage reached Step 5 (Build) honestly.

### Why This Needs Human Review
- The amendment rests on ONE organisation's case. This is proportional but thin.
- Pack Music is an existing Kamunity relationship — ecosystem confirmation bias is possible (the engine may weight evidence from known relationships higher than it should)
- The amendment doesn't contradict the core finding (capacity first for MOST communities) but it adds a carve-out. Carve-outs can expand if not held carefully.
- Whether "existing platforms are extractive by design" applies to cases beyond music streaming is untested.

### Review Question for Mike
**Does this amendment match your experience?** In your conversations with community organisations, is there a meaningful distinction between:
- (a) Groups that struggle with digital tools because of capacity/strategy/support gaps (capacity-first applies)
- (b) Groups that need something new because existing platforms are structurally hostile to their values (build applies)

If yes: the amendment holds and can be carried forward.
If no: the amendment should be scoped more narrowly (e.g., "applies only when the platform's business model is extractive, not when features are missing").
If you're not sure: hold the amendment and test it in community conversations.
