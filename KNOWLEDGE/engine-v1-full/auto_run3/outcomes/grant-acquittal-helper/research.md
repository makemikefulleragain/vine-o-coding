# Research: grant-acquittal-helper

## Search 1: "grant acquittal reporting tool software nonprofit community organisations Australia"
### Findings:
- **SurePact** — Australian grant management software. Enterprise-level, designed for both grantmakers and recipients. Includes milestone reports, financial tracking, acquittal reporting. Guided templates, dashboard, spreadsheet export. **Likely expensive — enterprise pricing, overkill for $5K-$50K community grants.**
- **Community Grants Hub** (communitygrants.gov.au) — Australian Government portal. States that non-audited financial acquittals have "no specific format" but must include income and expenditure statement confirming funds spent per grant agreement. Small grants ($5K-$50K) typically require non-audited acquittals.
- **DFAT** — Provides a grants acquittal template as a Word document.

## Search 2: "open source grant financial reporting expense tracking nonprofit"
### Findings:
- **No open-source grant acquittal tool found.** The space is served by:
  - Enterprise nonprofit budget software (Cube, Knack, Vena) — expensive, complex
  - General accounting tools with nonprofit features — overkill for acquittal
  - Open-source tools for NGOs (Zammad list) — ticketing, CRM, etc. Nothing for acquittal
- **opengrants.io** lists 12 grant management platforms — all commercial SaaS, most focused on grant discovery/application rather than acquittal
- Generic spreadsheet templates exist (Smartsheet, Vena) but require subscriptions

## Search 3: "grant acquittal template spreadsheet small community groups Australia free"
### Findings:
- **Smartsheet** — Free grant tracking templates (budget vs actual, expenditure monitoring). Requires Smartsheet subscription.
- **DFAT** — Word document acquittal template (free, basic)
- **Department of Finance** — Grants Toolkit with guidelines. Policy-focused, not a tool.
- **OpenGrants** — Grant budget templates for proposals, not acquittal
- **Vena** — Excel-based grant budget template. Requires platform.

## Key Finding: Non-audited acquittals are simple

The Australian Community Grants Hub explicitly states there is "no specific format" for non-audited financial acquittals. For small community grants ($5K-$50K), the acquittal is fundamentally:
1. What was budgeted per category
2. What was actually spent per category
3. Any variance explanation
4. Narrative of activities/outcomes
5. Signed declaration

This is a structured budget tracker with reporting — NOT a complex financial compliance system.
