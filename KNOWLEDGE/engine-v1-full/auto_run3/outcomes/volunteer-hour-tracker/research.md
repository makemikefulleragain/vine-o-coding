# Research: volunteer-hour-tracker

## Search 1: "volunteer hour tracking software free nonprofit open source community organisations"
### Findings:
- **Jibble** — 100% free forever, unlimited users. Volunteer hours tracking across multiple devices. Reports, attendance tracking. Explicit nonprofit focus: "We made Jibble free because nonprofits and volunteer teams deserve powerful tools—without the price tag." Testimonials from nonprofit anthropologists and digital marketing managers.
- **POINT** (pointapp.org) — Free all-in-one volunteer management platform. Volunteer time logging, impact tracking, reporting. "We track impact, so you don't have to."
- **Track It Forward** — Free volunteer hour logging platform. Specifically designed for logging volunteer hours. User-friendly first. Testimonial from **Australian Children's Education Charity**: "For the first time, we feel confident that all the amazing work our volunteers do is being captured and reported on correctly and efficiently."
- **YourVolunteers** — Free volunteer scheduling, management, and tracking. Database of volunteers, shift management, hour logging.

## Search 2: "volunteer management software time tracking hours logging nonprofit Australia free"
### Findings:
- **Track It Forward** — Confirmed: free, used by Australian nonprofits. Easy volunteer hour logging.
- **Jibble** — Confirmed: 100% free, reports, CSV/PDF export. Used by nonprofits.
- **Timecounts** — Modern volunteer management solution. Time logging, scheduled hours tracking, reports. "Essential to our volunteer program."
- **Clockify** — Free time tracker for nonprofits and volunteers. Reports break down time by project and person. Export as PDF, CSV, Excel.
