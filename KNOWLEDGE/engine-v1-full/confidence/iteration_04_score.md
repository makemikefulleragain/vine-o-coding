2. **Web listening heard peak bodies, not communities.** ALIKE WA speaks for 780 groups but we heard none of them. Volunteering WA supports thousands of volunteer-involving organisations but we heard none of them. The bias is structural to the method.

3. **FactoryK fit with Pack Music is speculative.** The suggestion that FactoryK could address developer dependency is logical but untested. Pack Music may already have development partners, or FactoryK's capacity may not match their technical needs. This must be explored in conversation, not assumed.

4. **INVOLVE evaluation is outstanding.** Before any recommendation involving volunteer management or coordination tools, INVOLVE must be evaluated. This is a Triage obligation, not optional.

5. **The "pitch vs listen" tension.** The relationship-specific briefs make conversations more informed but risk making them more transactional. Mike must calibrate: lead with curiosity and listening, not with what Kamunity can offer.
