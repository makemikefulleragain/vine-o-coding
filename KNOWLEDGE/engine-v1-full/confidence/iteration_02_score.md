## Flagged Uncertainties (per routing requirement)

1. **Australian voice gap:** Zero direct community voices across 2 iterations. The spec is grounded in sector reports and platform self-descriptions, not expressed community needs. This is the single largest uncertainty.

2. **Hylo viability unknown:** Values-aligned but no evidence of AU deployment, user retention, or financial sustainability beyond ~$85K/year donations. Cannot assess whether Hylo is a viable partner/platform without hands-on evaluation.

3. **Capacity-first framing may be incomplete:** The recommendation "capacity first, then tools" is supported by research but could be challenged by communities who say "we need a specific tool right now." We haven't heard from those communities.

4. **Central tension partially resolved:** Sequencing (capacity first, then evaluate tools) is a reasonable approach but may be experienced as a delay by communities with urgent coordination needs.

5. **Strengths-based vs. deficit framing:** The "Coordination Support Gap" framing, while honest, still positions communities as lacking something. A more strengths-based framing might emerge from community listening.
