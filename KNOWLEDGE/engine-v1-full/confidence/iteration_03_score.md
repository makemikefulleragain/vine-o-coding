## Flagged Items (Even at 88, Honest Flagging Required)

1. **No WA-specific data.** National statistics (Infoxchange) are used as proxy for WA context. Community conversations will reveal whether this holds locally.

2. **Post-conversation synthesis gap.** The package tells Mike how to have conversations but not how to systematically synthesise across 5-8 of them. A simple tagging template would help. (Addressed below in Phase 6.)

3. **Multicultural community access.** The package doesn't address communities with limited English proficiency or different cultural expectations around consultation. This is a known gap, not a blind spot.

4. **Follow-through commitment.** The conversation guide promises to "share back what you heard." This is a trust commitment. If Mike can't honour it, the conversations shouldn't happen.

5. **Timing.** The package assumes 5-8 conversations over 4-6 weeks. If community contacts are busy, unavailable, or unresponsive, the timeline may stretch. This is normal for community engagement, not a failure.
