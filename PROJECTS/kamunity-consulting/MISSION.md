# MISSION.md — Kamunity Consulting Website
## kamunityconsulting.com rebuild · Feb 2026

---

## Strategic Outcome

**Replace kamunityconsulting.com (currently a generic Wix site) with a static, two-door consulting website that speaks directly to the WA community sector, reflects Mike Fuller's actual voice and methodology, and converts interested visitors into paying clients.**

This is the revenue engine that funds the Kamunity ecosystem during the pre-grant phase. First paid engagement target: March 2026.

---

## Why This Matters

Mike Fuller has 30 years of QA, improvement, and innovation experience across mining, brewing, higher education, disability services, and community sector. He has delivered real results — $500k saved at RAC in 9 months, WA Health hackathon win, implemented youth placemaking, ALIKE WA strategy, three WALGA engagements. He is building post-capitalist community AI infrastructure on a balcony, pre-revenue, while planning a wedding.

The current website communicates none of this. It is generic. It sounds like every other consultant. It does not pass the Priya test. It is not earning its keep.

The new site has one job: when Priya (or her CEO) lands on it from a LinkedIn connection or targeted outreach email, she knows immediately whether Mike can help her, and she takes a next step.

---

## What "Done" Looks Like (North Star)

A person who needs Mike's help can:
1. **Land** — arrive from LinkedIn or email and know in 10 seconds if they're in the right place
2. **Recognise** — read 3 lines of copy and feel seen ("that's us")
3. **Choose** — pick the door that matches their reality
4. **Trust** — see one proof point that's specific, real, and sector-relevant
5. **Act** — take a low-friction next step without having to book a call with a stranger

This is the north star. Phase 1 gets us to a working front door. Later phases add depth.

---

## The Two Doors (Core Architecture)

### Door 1 — Fix the shit things
> *RAC. $500k found in 9 months. Nobody got fired.*

QA, process improvement, strategy, team alignment. For the competent, slightly exhausted person who knows what's broken and needs someone to fix it without drama. Red door energy.

**Services behind this door:**
- Quality Assurance audits and improvement planning
- Process mapping and waste identification
- Strategic alignment workshops
- Team and stakeholder experience design

### Door 2 — Do the impossible thing
> *WA Health hackathon. Won it by going outside and talking to actual humans.*

Design sprints, hackathons, innovation, AI integration. For the slightly feral person who has been told no too many times and needs a co-conspirator. Blue door energy.

**Services behind this door:**
- Design sprints and hackathons
- Innovation coaching
- AI readiness and integration (constitutional, sovereignty-first)
- Human-centred design for community impact

## The Ecosystem Context

This site is Door 1 of a three-site ecosystem:

| Site | Purpose | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| kamunityconsulting.com | **Get paid** — paid consulting front door | Priya, CEOs, board members ready to invest |
| K.ai | **Get connected** — community of practitioners | Community orgs, peers, collaborators |
| K.org | **Get it done** — tools, guides, stories, events | Everyone, free access, sovereign tools |

The consulting site feeds into the ecosystem. It is not isolated. But it does not dump the ecosystem on its front door — that's the Wix mistake. One job. One front door. Links to the rest in the footer.

---

## Who This Is For

**Primary — Priya**
Operations coordinator, 12-person NFP, Fremantle. Unofficial tech person. 3 minutes. Between meetings. Came from LinkedIn or a targeted email. Needs to feel seen before she clicks anything.

**Secondary — The CEO**
More ROI-focused. More time-pressured. Cares about the sector framing. Arrived via Version 4 copy ("cutting hours, reducing services, doing it anyway"). Wants proof before conversation.

**Not for:** Generic corporate sector. Large government agencies. Anyone who already has a digital transformation vendor.

---

## What This Is NOT

Not a portfolio site. Not a CV. Not a capabilities statement. Not a features list. Not a "here's all the things Mike does" dump. Not a thought leadership blog (that's K.org). Not a community tool (that's K.ai/K.org). Not another Wix template with stock photos of handshakes.

It IS the bridge between "I think we need help" and "I'm talking to Mike."

---

## Measuring Success

**Immediate (Phase 1-2):**
- Site is live, passes the Priya test (10 seconds, above the fold, no scroll required)
- Both door versions routing correctly
- At least one "I'm interested" mechanism working

**Medium term:**
- First inbound inquiry from site (not outreach)
- First paid engagement traced to site visit
- Priya test passed by at least 3 real community sector workers (informal testing)

**Not measured by:**
- Page views
- Bounce rate
- SEO ranking (yet)
- Social shares
