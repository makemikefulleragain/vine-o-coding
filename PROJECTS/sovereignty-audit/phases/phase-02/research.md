## Key Findings

### 1. Microsoft 365 NFP Licensing Changes (Critical — July 2025)
- **Source:** Peppermint IT (Australian MSP), Out of Site AU
- **What happened:** From 1 July 2025, Microsoft ended free Business Premium and Office 365 E1 grants for nonprofits
- **What's still free:** Microsoft 365 Business Basic (up to 300 users) — web/mobile Office apps, Outlook, 1TB OneDrive, Teams, SharePoint
- **What's gone:** Desktop Office apps, advanced security (Defender, Intune), device management
- **Impact:** Many Australian NFPs relied on free Premium tier. Now must budget for paid plans or switch to alternatives
- **Takeaway for us:** This is THE most urgent toolkit topic. A guide on "What the Microsoft changes mean for your org" is immediately valuable. Google Workspace for Nonprofits (free up to 100 users) is a key alternative to highlight.

### 2. Nonprofit AI Policy (Only 15% Have One)
- **Source:** Candid.org (2025 AI Equity Project data)
- **Key pattern:** Only 15% of surveyed US/Canadian nonprofits have implemented an AI policy
- **4 essentials for a responsible AI use policy:**
  1. Purpose and scope — what this covers and who it applies to
  2. Values and commitments — privacy, transparency, equity, human oversight
  3. Prompting and data-handling norms — what's safe to enter, what's not
  4. When NOT to use AI — high-stakes decisions, crisis comms, case notes, sensitive data
- **Best practices:**
  - Keep it simple and adaptable
  - Write in plain language (not legal language)
  - Design for real roles (fundraising, programs, finance, operations)
  - Don't make it fear-based (drives AI use underground)
  - Start with trust, not prediction of every scenario
- **Takeaway for us:** A one-page AI safety starter template is extremely high-value. 85% of nonprofits don't have this. We can provide a fill-in-the-blanks template.

### 3. Nonprofit Software Discounts (Widely Unknown)
- **Sources:** TechSoup blog, Microsoft Nonprofits, Reddit r/nonprofit, Arts Midwest, TruAdvantage
- **Key programs most orgs don't know about:**
  - Google for Nonprofits — free Workspace (up to 100 users), $10k/month Ad Grants
  - Microsoft Nonprofits — free Business Basic (300 users), discounted Premium
  - Canva for Nonprofits — free Canva Pro
  - Slack for Nonprofits — 85% discount on Pro plan
  - Zoom for Nonprofits — 50% discount
  - TechSoup — aggregator for dozens of discounts
  - Many others (Asana, Monday.com, 1Password, etc.)
- **Common mistakes:** Applying directly instead of through TechSoup, misconfigured licenses, unused credits
- **Takeaway for us:** A "Nonprofit Discount Directory" is immediately actionable. Many orgs are literally overpaying because they don't know these exist.

### 4. Common NFP Technology Pain Points
- **From research synthesis:**
  - Email/productivity platform lock-in (Google vs Microsoft decision)
  - No asset register of digital tools
  - Security basics missing (MFA, backup admin)
  - AI tools being used informally without guidelines
  - Staff turnover losing institutional knowledge of how tools work
  - Grant reporting requiring cost documentation

## Content Format Research

Based on Priya persona (30 minutes/week for digital stuff):
- **Checklists** > articles (scannable, actionable)
- **Decision trees** > comparisons (reduces cognitive load)
- **Templates** > guides (immediately usable)
- **One page** > multi-page (respects time constraints)
