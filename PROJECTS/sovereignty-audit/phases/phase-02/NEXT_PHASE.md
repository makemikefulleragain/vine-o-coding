# Next Phase Recommendation

**From:** Phase 2 (The Free Toolkit)
**Recommended next:** Phase 3 (The Services Layer + Trust & Security + Findability)

---

## Why Phase 3 Next

Phases 1 and 2 deliver the free value (audit + toolkit). Phase 3 makes the project sustainable by adding the services layer, trust elements, and making the site findable. This aligns with the PHASE_QUEUE.md specification which combines services, trust/security, and findability into Phase 3.

## What Phase 3 Should Include (per PHASE_QUEUE.md)

### Services Layer
- Transparent pricing page with three tiers
- NFP/community rate and corporate rate
- Booking/contact flow
- Clear "here's what you get, what it costs, the process"

### Trust & Security
- About page (team, mission, values)
- Privacy policy
- FAQ
- Data handling transparency

### Findability
- SEO fundamentals (meta tags, structured data)
- llms.txt for AI crawler legibility
- Links back to kamunity.ai, kamunity.org, kamunityconsulting.com
- Content foundations for social media

## What to Research in Phase 3
- What pricing models work for NFP consulting services?
- What trust signals matter most for community orgs choosing a consultant?
- What SEO/AI-legibility techniques are current best practice?
- How to implement llms.txt and JSON-LD structured data
