# Next Phase Recommendation

**From:** Phase 1 (The Free Audit)
**Recommended next:** Phase 2 (The Free Toolkit)

---

## Why Phase 2 Next

Phase 1 delivers a sovereignty score with recommendations, but the recommendations are text-only. Phase 2 turns those recommendations into actionable guides — "Your email is locked in → here's how to evaluate alternatives."

This follows the original phase queue and is validated by research:
- Infoxchange data shows NFPs want practical, actionable resources
- The audit creates a "now what?" moment that the toolkit addresses
- Providing free value builds trust before any paid services are offered

## What Phase 2 Should Include

Based on Phase 1 learnings:
1. **Per-dimension guides** linked from the results page
2. **Tool comparison resources** for common NFP tools (email, file storage, project management)
3. **AI safety starter kit** — the most urgent new need for community orgs
4. **NFP discount directory** — many orgs don't know about nonprofit pricing

## What to Research in Phase 2
- What specific tool transitions do Australian NFPs most commonly face?
- What existing free resources already cover this? (Don't rebuild what exists)
- What format works best — articles, checklists, decision trees?
