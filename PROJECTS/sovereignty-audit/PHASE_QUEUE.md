## The Queue

### Phase 1: The Free Audit ("Make it real"): 8-12 questions, takes 2 minutes
**Goal:** The Free Audit ("Make it real"): 8-12 questions, takes 2 minutes. Covers: what tools do you use, who owns your data, could you leave tomorrow, how much are you paying, who has admin access, are you using AI tools and how. Produces a "Sovereignty Score" (not a grade — a map) with three categories: green/amber/red across dimensions like Data Ownership, Vendor Lock-in, Cost Transparency, AI Readiness. Each category gets 1-2 specific recommendations. All client-side, no data transmitted.
**Why first:** This is the most important thing to get right before anything else.

### Phase 2: The Free Toolkit ("Make it useful"): Based on audit results, serve up relevant guides
**Goal:** The Free Toolkit ("Make it useful"): Based on audit results, serve up relevant guides. "Your email is locked in → here's how to evaluate alternatives." "You're paying $X/month for tools that have free sovereign equivalents → here's a comparison." Prompt kits for using AI safely. Getting-started guides for key transitions. This is the freemium content layer — generous, practical, builds trust.
**Why second:** Build on Phase 1's foundation with the next most valuable feature.

### Phase 3: The Services Layer ("Make it sustainable"): For orgs that want help, transparent pricing page
**Goal:** The Services Layer ("Make it sustainable"): For orgs that want help, transparent pricing page. Three tiers of engagement matching the consulting packages. NFP/community rate clearly shown. Corporate rate clearly shown (and yes, higher). Booking/contact flow. Testimonials as they come in. "Here's exactly what you get, here's exactly what it costs, here's the process."
Make It Trustworthy & secure — about page, privacy policy, FAQ, data deletion. ensure all security vectors are known and eliminated, mitigated or accepted with full informed consent and a record is maintained throughout the build to ensure a secure and stable build for the user. it is of no value if the user's data is immediately harvested and sold or the site is hijacked, hacked or taken down
Make it findable: create a secure admin centre to maintain and add content to the content section like testimonials, blogs, case studies, podcasts, video clips, etc. Create contextually aligned and honest seed content. we have intial clients, 30 years of experince and small but dedicated team plus growing network of supporters. we are doing this in a local government peak body as we speak, building a tailored kamunity for The Pack Music Australia and speaking to other commuity peak bodies and micro-NFPs serving small communities that are doing it tough. we just need to share and grow together.
This section will also create content for different social media formats based on an input theme or concept i.e. new tools or client case studies and other news from the Kamunity. The site must also be legible to AI and bot crawlers and link back to kamunity.ai and kamunity.org and kamunityconsulting.com. use things like llm.text files and JSON manifestos, or other techniques, because some ai chats can not render the sites as they are client-side or server-side... not sure but the AI must be able to scan and understand the site and the ecosystem it is connected to (NOT kamunity in Sweden, the European Reddit or kamunity.io the finance thing) 
**Why third:** After the core is working, add depth and polish.

### Phase 4+: Expand Based on Evidence
