# MISSION.md — Kamunity Reflection

---

## Strategic Outcome

Community organisations in WA can discover what they're actually for — and find others facing the same fork — through a 5-minute Kai conversation that leaves them with one useful thing in hand and a question lingering that they didn't have before.

---

## Why This Matters

Most community tools make organisations more legible to funders, government, and platforms. None make them more legible to themselves. The questions that matter most — "What are we for? Who else needs what we have? What do we know that no system can replicate?" — never get asked because the org is in survival mode and the tools don't go there.

Scarcity in the community sector is mostly an information problem, not a resource problem. The value is already there — 99% latent. Kamunity Reflection is community self-perception infrastructure: it helps the commons see itself.

---

## What Done Looks Like (North Star)

- An org leader can arrive with 5 minutes, answer one question, get one immediately useful reframe, and leave with something they didn't have before — even if they never come back.
- Kai surfaces value exchange possibilities (swaps, loops, chains) between orgs that didn't know they were each other's resource.
- "Like them" stories from the peer corpus give orgs a mirror that isn't expert validation — it's peer witnessing.
- The decision history ("three orgs in Perth mental health faced this fork — here's what each chose and what happened") is populated from public signals, not user data.
- Orgs that engage leave slightly more legible to themselves than before. The shift happens in the residue.

---

## Who This Is For

**Primary User:** Priya — operations coordinator at a 12-person NFP. She has 45 minutes between meetings, no cognitive overhead for another audit, and needs one thing she can actually use.

**Also Serves:**
- ALIKE WA (peer support peak body)
- Activate Mental Health (sector coordination)
- The Pack Music (small arts/community org)
- The "already-weird" orgs that have asked the identity question through some other crisis and are ready to name it

## What This Is NOT

- Not a sovereignty calculator (that's the Audit tool — this is a companion, not a replacement)
- Not a therapy or counselling service
- Not a data extraction tool — nothing the user shares is stored, sold, or used against them
- Not for organisations that already know exactly what they're for and just need better software
