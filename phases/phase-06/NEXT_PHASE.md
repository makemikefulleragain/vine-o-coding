# Phase 6 → Next Steps

**Date:** 2026-02-15

---

## What Phase 6 Taught Us

1. **Truthfulness must be verified by real users, not self-audited.** The "5 simple questions" error persisted through 5 phases until a real tester caught it.
2. **Maya wants visual richness beyond animation.** The step-through flow partially satisfies her, but illustrated characters/diagrams may be needed.
3. **The feedback widget enables continuous improvement.** Every visitor can now contribute structured feedback without friction.
4. **Desktop spacing matters.** Generous mobile padding creates scroll fatigue on desktop.

## Possible Future Work (evidence-dependent)

- **Illustrated characters** — Maya/Brian visual personas on key pages
- **Accessibility audit** — ARIA landmarks, reduced-motion, screen reader testing
- **Netlify Forms verification** — confirm submissions arrive after deploy
- **Richer animation** — animated SVG illustrations of the build process
- **Feedback analysis** — review submissions, identify patterns, prioritize next work
- **Performance** — lazy loading for case study page (heaviest page)
- **i18n** — if non-English feedback appears

## Recommendation

Deploy, verify Netlify Forms works, and let the feedback widget drive Phase 7 scope. The site now has:
- 4 pages (landing, widget, case study, about)
- Animated methodology flow
- Anonymous feedback collection
- Truthful copy throughout
- Trust pages (FAQ, Privacy, Terms)

Further phases should be driven by feedback widget submissions, not assumptions.
