# Phase 2 → Phase 3 Proposal

**Date:** 2026-02-15

---

## What Phase 2 Taught Us

1. **The widget is the core product and it works.** Six steps, companion examples, Brian notes, document generation, ZIP download, and opening prompt — all compile and build cleanly. The acceptance gate needs human verification but the architecture is sound.

2. **The companion examples are the secret weapon.** Showing both a real project (Grants Hub) and a relatable one (Recipe Remix) at every step teaches by example, not by instruction. This pattern should carry into the case study.

3. **Brian's lean-in sections add real depth without clutter.** The `<details>` pattern works perfectly. Each note references real theory (Lean, jobs-to-be-done, Agile, Andon cord). These are seeds for the Phase 3 deep dives.

4. **The "What to do next" section creates natural demand for Phase 3.** After completing the widget, users who succeed will want to understand the methodology better. Users who struggle will want the case study to see the process in action.

5. **The generated RUNNER.md references the phase loop but doesn't explain it.** Phase 3's case study showing the grants-hub going through actual phases (including the failure) makes the loop tangible.

## Proposed Phase 3: Make It Learnable

**Goal:** The case study, deep dives, and methodology depth — for people who want to understand why the process works, not just use it.

**This aligns with the original Phase 3 in PHASE_QUEUE.md.** No queue change needed.

### What Should Be Built

1. **Interactive grants-hub timeline** — 8 phases visualized, clickable, showing key decisions and outcomes. The Phase 2 failure and recovery is the star of the show.

2. **Brian's deep dives** — expandable methodology articles:
   - Why phases are hypotheses (not fixed plans)
   - Confidence scoring (what it catches, when to reassess)
   - The Andon cord / Review & Reflect concept
   - Human-AI collaboration model (who owns what)

3. **Methodology report** — downloadable PDF or markdown document. The professional reference for teachers, workshop leaders, curious developers.

4. **Foundation document templates** — annotated versions showing what each section is for and why it matters.

### Research Questions for Phase 3

- How do interactive timelines/case studies work best for teaching?
- What depth of methodology documentation do workshop facilitators need?
- Should the case study be a separate page or integrated into the landing page?
- How much grants-hub phase detail is too much? (balance depth vs overwhelm)

### Risk

Phase 3 is content-heavy. The risk is producing a wall of text that nobody reads. Research must validate the format (timeline? accordion? scrollytelling?) before building.

## Queue Status

No changes to PHASE_QUEUE.md needed. Phase 3 as written is exactly right.
