## MEETING 1 — ALIKE WA CEO

**Who:** ALIKE WA — Advocacy, Learning, Independence, Knowledge, Empowerment for people with disability
**What:** CEO meeting — demo + relationship building
**Goal:** Leave them knowing Kamunity exists, what it does, and why it's relevant to their members
**Ask:** Discover their digital challenges → position Kamunity tools as free resources → explore deeper partnership

### Context to Know

- ALIKE WA is a WA disability peak body — represents people with disability, their families, carers
- Their members need digital tools but often have low digital literacy, limited budgets, and high trust requirements
- NDIS has created enormous administrative burden on member organisations — AI could help or harm depending on implementation
- Accessibility is non-negotiable in their world: WCAG 2.1 AA is the baseline
- Any AI tool touching their clients faces immediate "is it safe?" scrutiny — rightly so

### Why Kamunity Is Relevant to ALIKE

- **The Audit** (2 min free) → helps orgs discover vendor lock-in, data exposure, digital costs → directly relevant to NFPs in the disability sector managing client data
- **AI Readiness** (5 min free) → helps orgs understand where they are on AI — not just "should we use it" but "how ready are we to use it safely"
- **The Constitution** → explicit anti-dark-patterns principle (Principle 6), no exploitation of vulnerability (Principle 8) — speaks their language
- **The Safety Checklist** → leave-behind that their staff can use immediately

### 5-Minute Demo Flow

1. Open **kamunity.org** — "This is Kai. A community AI wayfinder. Not a chatbot. Different thing."
2. Ask Kai: "What should a disability service provider know about AI?" — let Kai answer, shows the tone
3. Show **AI Readiness Assessment** — "This is for your member orgs. 5 minutes. Free. No data collection."
4. Show **Sovereignty Audit** — "This is the digital health check. 2 minutes. Shows what's exposed."
5. Show **AI Safety Checklist** (printed) — "This is for frontline staff. Anyone in their organisation can use it today."

### Questions to Ask (in this order)

1. "What's the biggest digital challenge your member organisations are facing right now?"
2. "Are any of your members already using AI tools — formally or informally?"
3. "What would make an AI tool trustworthy enough to recommend to your members?"
4. "Is there a particular member org we could pilot with?"

### Framing Points

- "WCAG 2.1 AA compliance is constitutionally required — not a bolt-on."
- "Anti-dark-patterns is one of our 11 inviolable principles."
- "Everything is free to community organisations at this stage."
- "We're not selling software. We're building infrastructure."

### What You're NOT Doing

- Not pitching a paid product (yet)
- Not asking for anything specific beyond "would you share these tools with your members?"
- Not making promises about features that don't exist

### Possible Outcomes

| Outcome | How to Handle |
|---|---|
| "Can we share this with members?" | ✅ Yes — give them audit + readiness URLs + checklist |
| "Can you present at our event?" | ✅ Yes — ask for details, follow up within 24hrs |
| "We want a workshop" | ✅ Yes — $1,500–$2,500 NFP rate, propose dates |
| "I need to think about it" | ✅ Fine — follow up in 48hrs, send audit link |
| "We already have a tool" | Ask what it is. Learn. Don't compete — complement. |

### Follow-Up (within 24hrs)

- Email: reference specific thing they mentioned
- Include: audit link, readiness link, AI Safety Checklist PDF
- Offer: "Happy to run a 30-minute AI orientation for your team — free"

## MEETING 2 — Activate Mental Health Board

**Who:** Activate Mental Health — WA mental health organisation
**What:** Board candidacy interview (or advisory role discussion)
**Goal:** Demonstrate you understand MH + AI safety deeply, that you add distinct value to their board
**Ask:** A seat at the table — to bridge "AI is scary" and "here's how we make it safe"

### Why You're Different on This Board

Most board candidates bring finance, legal, or sector expertise. You bring something rare:

> "I build constitutional AI specifically for community organisations, with explicit safety guardrails. I've done the safety research, the threat modelling, the crisis protocol work. I'm not here to evangelise AI — I'm here to help you navigate it safely."

### Key Talking Points

**On AI risk in mental health:**
- Meta's Messenger chatbot crisis study: 33% failure rate on crisis scenarios in 2023 research
- AI crisis tools that attempt therapy rather than referring to humans cause harm
- The risk isn't AI being malicious — it's AI being confidently wrong at the worst possible moment
- Your work: Kai has WA-specific crisis resources hardcoded. Not AI-generated. Tested.

**On your governance value:**
- You've written and published a constitution for an AI system (with amendment mechanisms)
- You've built and documented a safety threat model
- You've run systematic prompt injection testing
- You have a lived understanding of what responsible AI deployment actually requires — technically and ethically

**On the opportunity:**
- MH sector is going to adopt AI tools regardless. The question is whether they do it safely.
- The board that builds a principled position now is ahead of the organisations that will scramble when something goes wrong.
- You can help them ask the right questions before any AI tool is adopted.

### Key Questions to Ask

1. "What's your current position on AI tools in mental health delivery?"
2. "Have any of your member or partner organisations started using AI tools with clients?"
3. "What would a principled AI adoption policy look like for your organisation?"
4. "What does the board most need that it doesn't currently have?"

### What You're Bringing to the Board

| Gap You Fill | How |
|---|---|
| Technical AI literacy | Can translate between "AI is magic" and "here's what it actually does" |
| Safety methodology | Constitutional approach, threat modelling, testing protocols |
| Community tech perspective | Not enterprise AI — community-scale, low-resource context |
| Network | ALIKE, Spacecubed, WACOSS connections |
| Current build | kamunity.org live — can demo a responsibly-built AI right now |

### The Ask (explicitly)

Be direct: "I'd like to contribute to this board specifically to help you navigate AI adoption safely. That's the gap I see, and it's the contribution I'm best positioned to make."

### If Asked About Conflicts of Interest

"Kamunity is pre-revenue. I'm building public-good tools. I have no commercial relationships with AI vendors. If that changes, I'll disclose it immediately. My constitution explicitly prohibits exploiting community vulnerability for commercial gain."
